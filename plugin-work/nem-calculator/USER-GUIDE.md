# User Guide

## Calculator Flow

The calculator is designed for residential Domestic Tariff A users.

### Step 1: Enter Monthly Bill

Provide your average monthly TNB bill in RM.

### Step 2: Choose Location

Select the closest supported Johor or Melaka location.

### Step 3: Choose Payment Method

Available options:

- Cash
- Loan
- Credit Installment

Current estimates use the same installation cost model for all three options.

### Step 4: Choose Voltage Level

- Single Phase: up to 4kWac
- Three Phase: up to 10kWac

### Step 5: Review Results

The calculator shows:

- Estimated system capacity
- Monthly generation
- Monthly and annual savings
- Payback period
- 25-year savings projection
- Self-consumption and export-to-grid breakdown

## Lead Capture

If enabled by the site administrator, users can submit their details after reviewing the result:

- Name
- Email
- Phone number

## Admin Features

Administrators can:

- View calculation totals and savings trends
- Review and update leads
- Export leads to CSV
- Adjust branding colors and CTA content
