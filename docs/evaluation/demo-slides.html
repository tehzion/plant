KANB AI Agropreneur

Checking AI crop diagnosis against public plant-disease datasets

Farmers photograph a sick plant; the AI names the likely problem and safe next steps. We built a pipeline that measures how often it agrees with expert-labelled reference images, and used it to find and fix a weakness.

Pipeline

Reference data and AI predictions kept separate

PlantVillage / PlantDoc→Object storage (checksummed)→Neon PostgreSQL→Farmer assessment API→Evaluation results in Neon→Admin dashboard
500reference images (472 PlantVillage, 28 PlantDoc)
14crops · 36 dataset labels · 150 healthy / 350 diseased
168completed real AI assessments across 86 images

Disease labels are never sent to the AI. The same endpoint farmers use performs every assessment.

Finding

The AI often misidentified the crop

  • Farmers could only pick a broad group such as “Vegetables”.
  • 6 of 20 test images got the wrong crop: tomato read as bean or cabbage, or “unknown crop”.
  • Without the right crop the AI cannot narrow its diagnosis, so it fell back to generic answers like “Leaf Spot”.

Fix: an optional “Which crop?” choice (in English, Malay and Chinese) that the AI treats as the farmer’s stated crop.

Result 2 · safety

Fewer confident treatments for the wrong disease

  • Found: confident “Early Blight” treatment advice on late-blight and leaf-mould leaves; the AI never considered either.
  • Fix: on tomato and potato, treatment advice now requires the look-alike blights to be compared; otherwise an expert reviews first.
