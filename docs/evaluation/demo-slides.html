KANB AI Agropreneur

An AI plant doctor for Malaysian farmers, tested against public research datasets

A farmer photographs a sick plant and chooses the crop. The AI names the likely problem, says what to check, gives safe next steps in Malay, English or Chinese, and suggests KANB products.

How we used the datasets

Tested the way farmers use it, then improved and re-tested

75,000+labelled photos in 7 public datasets: PlantVillage, PlantDoc, padi, chilli, papaya, mango
Blindeach photo sent through the farmer API with the crop chosen; the label is never sent
100photos held back and never used while improving the app

We did not train our own model. We used the datasets to find where the AI fails, improve the app around it, and prove each change on the same photos.
