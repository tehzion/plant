Meet Decisions.
A second opinion, built in.

After the main AI makes its call, a second model looks at the same photo and answers precise questions. It doesn't write an essay. It answers yes, no or unclear.

Step one

The diagnosis

GPT-5.4-mini proposes the most likely problem and its look-alikes.

Typed questions

Step three

A safer answer

If the two disagree, the case is marked for a closer look before anyone sprays.

It can only add caution

Decisions can ask for review. It can never remove a warning, raise confidence or unlock a treatment.

It catches false comfort

If one model says "treat" and the other sees a healthy plant, that conflict is caught.

Your consent first

It only runs for farmers who opted in to help improve the AI, with strict limits on time and cost.
